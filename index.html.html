Riverbend Elementary rain garden

Learn how one project turns SRCs into revenue.

You’re playing as an SRC aggregator who helped install a 2,800 sq ft rain garden at Riverbend Elementary in the non-tidal MS4.

This rain garden:

  • Captures roof and parking lot runoff before it reaches DC’s waters,
  • Creates a small habitat patch for pollinators and a “living lab” for students, and
  • Generates a finite amount of Stormwater Retention Credits (SRCs) – about 20,000 SRCs over its crediting period.

This trainer will show how:

  • Deal size (how many of those SRCs you sell in this transaction),
  • Location & timing (non-tidal vs tidal, years 1–6 vs 7–12),
  • Agreement type with DOEE, and
  • Your SRC price on the market

all work together to shape your revenue, DOEE’s support, and what buyers are willing to pay.
